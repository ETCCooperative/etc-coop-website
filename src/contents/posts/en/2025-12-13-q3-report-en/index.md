We are pleased to share [ETC Cooperative's Board Package for Q3](https://etccooperative.org/etc-cooperative-q3-2025-en.pdf).

Highlights:

 - **Development and Infrastructure:** In Q3, the ETC Cooperative focused on the stability and reliability of core-geth. Work emphasized monitoring, maintenance, and selective hardening. No major feature rollouts shipped this quarter, but continuous upkeep kept the network dependable for users and developers.
 - **Core Team Expansion:** A hiring initiative funded by ETCGrantsDAO (Bitmain) began to onboard two core developers to re-accelerate protocol progress. The collaboration scope was defined, job descriptions published, and the first applicants are being reviewed.
 - **Comms and Marketing:** As previously announced, our Marketing Manager, Andrew Dick, stepped down from his role as of June 15 2025. The marketing function at the ETC Cooperative has been discontinued until further notice.
 - **Financials:** The organization continues in maintenance mode with spending focused on essential operations: wages, infrastructure, and overhead costs.
