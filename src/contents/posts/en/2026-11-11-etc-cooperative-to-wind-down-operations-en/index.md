Ethereum Classic Cooperative, Inc. has begun the process of winding down its operations, with most of the dissolution requirements expected to be completed by the end of 2026.

Since 2017, ETC Cooperative has supported the development and adoption of Ethereum Classic by funding protocol and infrastructure development, developer tools, education and community initiatives. We are proud of what the community has built together over that time.

## What this means

 - **Grants:** ETC Cooperative is no longer accepting grant applications or making new funding commitments. Existing grantees will be contacted directly about any work in progress.
 - **Infrastructure:** The Cooperative is in the process of making arrangements for the public infrastructure it has supported, including network nodes and the Blockscout block explorer, to continue running for a transition period after the wind-down. This gives the community time to plan for its long-term support.
 - **Team:** As part of the wind-down, Diego López León's role concluded on November 7, 2026. We are deeply grateful to Diego for all of his contributions over the years.

## Questions and outstanding matters

Anyone with questions, or with outstanding invoices or other matters involving ETC Cooperative, should contact [alison@etccooperative.org](mailto:alison@etccooperative.org) by December 6, 2026.

Thank you to everyone who has contributed to ETC Cooperative and to Ethereum Classic. The network, and the community that sustains it, will continue onward.

*Ethereum Classic Cooperative, Inc.*
